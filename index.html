Leri Automotive · Internship, summer 2026

1954 Corvette Restomod

A full restomod built alongside John Leri. My main part was the sheet-metal access panel over the car's electronics, with a recessed channel around its edge sized for a 5 mm LED strip — so the panel is the light. I measured the car, modeled the panel in SolidWorks, fabricated it, and was the second set of hands through the rest of the assembly. I also shot and edited the film on the finished build.

About

Pitt · MechE · GPA 3.70

I'm a mechanical engineering student at Pitt's Swanson School, based between Erie and Pittsburgh. Most of what I actually know came from taking something apart and then needing it to work again.

Really, I just love well-designed things. A part where somebody clearly thought about how it would feel to use, and not only whether it would work, is the kind of thing I notice and the kind of thing I want to make. Cars are where that started and where most of the work here comes from, but it isn't only cars — it's as true of a printed clip or a housing that fits in your hand as it is of a body panel.

What I like is the whole span of it: measuring the real thing, modeling it, making the part, and then finding out what I got wrong. I'm early in this and there's plenty I haven't done yet, so I'm keeping an open mind about where I end up — I'd be glad to do this kind of work anywhere it's useful.
